# Reviewer Suite Domain Glossary

Academic study portal and exam preparation workspace for BatStateU computing students, engineered for Apple HIG mobile ergonomics and distraction-free academic focus.

## Language

**Course**:
A distinct academic subject (e.g., CS 312 Mobile Computing, IT 321 Human-Computer Interaction, GEd 104 The Contemporary World).
_Avoid_: Subject, class, module (when referring to the top-level course)

**Term Exam Scope**:
A primary academic testing milestone within a course (Prelim, Midterm, Final).
_Avoid_: Semester, quarter, exam mode

**Study Tool**:
One of the four core specialized tools within a Term Exam Scope (`Cheat Sheet`, `Flashcards`, `Mock Exam`, `Guide Questions`).
_Avoid_: Feature, tab, gadget, widget

**Think-First Cooldown**:
A 10-second pedagogical lockout period with an animated fill-up bar preventing instant hint access, enforcing independent cognitive retrieval before assistance is offered.
_Avoid_: Timer delay, lock, penalty timer

**Shuffled Retry**:
A dynamic re-ordering of examination items upon restart or targeted review of missed items to prevent positional memorization.
_Avoid_: Randomizer, shuffle mode

**Navigation Drawer**:
The mobile-accessible slide-over sheet or desktop sidebar displaying the hierarchical Course ➔ Term tree.
_Avoid_: Hamburger popup, sidebar overlay

**Bottom Control Dock**:
The sticky, thumb-friendly segmented navigation bar on mobile allowing instant switching between the 4 Study Tools within the active Term.
_Avoid_: Bottom dock buttons, footer nav

**Mastery**:
The persistent state indicating whether a student has marked a flashcard as known or passed an exam bank threshold.
_Avoid_: Progress score, gamification level, XP

**Pointer Focus**:
Content and exam objectives specifically emphasized by official faculty examination pointers.
_Avoid_: Hot topics, cheat items, leaks
